Sources and selection rules

The daily scout reads public RSS and Atom feeds, keeps the headline and link, then scores odd wording. It does not copy article bodies.

How a headline gets picked

Look for odd wording

The script scores words like mystery, accidentally, bizarre, tiny, giant, and other straight-faced signals.

Leave harm alone

Stories about death, assault, disasters, war, and similar heavy subjects are filtered out before scoring.

Link back

Each card links to the source. The site stores the headline, source, date, URL, and a short note about the wording.

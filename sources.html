Sources and selection rules

The daily scout reads public RSS and Atom feeds, keeps the headline and link, then scores odd wording. It does not copy article bodies.

How a headline gets picked

Put Darwin first

NT News gets the strongest source boost. If it has a usable odd headline, it should naturally float near the top of the bill.

Look for odd wording

The script scores words like mystery, accidentally, bizarre, tiny, giant, mud crab, mullet, and other straight-faced signals.

Leave harm alone

Stories about death, assault, disasters, war, pandemic claims, psychic warnings, abductions, and conspiracy-scare phrasing are filtered out before scoring.
